Privacy

Your photos stay on your device. Text is read inside your browser. Photos are never uploaded.

Your words stay on your device. Scans and saved words are stored in your browser. Clearing your browser data deletes them. There is no account and no sync.

What leaves your device. When you tap a word, that single word (not your photo or sentence) is sent to Wiktionary, and to the MyMemory translation service if Wiktionary has no entry. The first scan also downloads the Dutch language data and the page loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services can see your IP address.

No tracking. No analytics, no ads, no cookies of our own, and nothing is sold or shared.
